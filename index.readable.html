Questions

Everything you were about to ask.

What is Insidr?

Insidr is a sports tipster platform. Bettors find expert tipsters, check their verified records, and subscribe to get their premium picks. Tipsters build a following and earn from subscriptions.

Is Insidr a betting site? Can I bet real money here?

No — Insidr is not a bookmaker and you never wager real money on the platform. Tips are opinions, tracked in virtual units for a fair, comparable record. You place any real bets with your own bookmaker, wherever you choose.

How do I know a tipster's stats are real?

Every ROI and profit figure is computed automatically from settled picks. A tipster can't inflate a record or hide losing bets — the numbers you see are the numbers that happened.

How much does it cost?

Browsing tipsters and their public records is free. Each tipster sets their own monthly subscription price to unlock premium picks, and you can cancel any time.

Can I become a tipster and earn?

Yes. Once you've built a qualifying record you can turn on payouts through Stripe and earn from monthly subscribers — plus direct tips from supporters. Insidr takes a small platform fee; the rest is yours.

What if a tipster I subscribed to goes quiet?

If a tipster posts no picks during your billing cycle, you're eligible for a full refund of that period. The app surfaces the option to you automatically when it applies.
